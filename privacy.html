Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Jarvis is a personal app used only by its owner. It accesses the owner's Gmail and Google Calendar solely to show, summarize and manage the owner's own mail and events.

Data is processed on the owner's computer and in the owner's private automation account. Mail and calendar content may be sent to Anthropic's Claude API to write summaries. No data is sold, shared with third parties for advertising, or used for any other purpose.

Jarvis's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
